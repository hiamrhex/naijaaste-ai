# Roadmap

This document outlines the planned development and future direction of NaijaTaste AI.

## Vision

NaijaTaste AI aims to become the most intelligent and user-friendly restaurant discovery platform in Nigeria, powered by AI and designed for local preferences and conditions.

## Current Status

NaijaTaste AI is currently in **beta release** with core functionality including:
- Restaurant discovery and recommendations
- AI-powered chat interface
- User authentication and preferences
- Map integration
- Progressive Web App capabilities

### Technical Risks
- **AI service reliability**: Implement fallback systems

- **Performance issues**: Regular monitoring and optimization
- **Security vulnerabilities**: Regular audits and updates

### Business Risks
- **Market competition**: Differentiation through AI and local focus
- **User adoption**: Effective marketing and user experience
- **Revenue generation**: Multiple streams and business model validation
- **Regulatory compliance**: Stay updated on regulations

### Operational Risks
- **Team scaling**: Hire strategically and maintain culture
- **Technology changes**: Stay current with trends
- **Partnership management**: Strong relationships and contracts
- **Quality control**: Testing and monitoring systems
