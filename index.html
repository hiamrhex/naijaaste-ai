How it works

Three steps. Sixty seconds. Dinner sorted.

  1. 01

    Chat

    Say where you are and what you're craving — in English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo or Pidgin.

  2. 02

    We learn your taste

    The agent builds your persona: city, budget, spice, ambience, dietary needs.

  3. 03

    Get your shortlist

Features

An agent, not a directory.

🤖 ReAct agent + 4 tools

Gemini reasons in a 6-step loop — searching, recommending, reviewing and saving preferences — with every step traced on screen.

🧬 Taste persona

Your profile sharpens with every chat: spice tolerance, budget, ambience, dietary flags. It remembers, so you don't repeat yourself.

📍 Near-you search

Haversine-ranked spots around your live location, each with one-tap Google Maps directions.

❤️ Favorites that follow you

Heart a spot once — it's on every device you're signed into.

✍️ Reviews in your voice

Generate vivid reviews written from your persona, matched to the restaurant's vibe.

🔐 Serious auth

15-minute JWTs, rotating refresh tokens, bcrypt hashing, email-OTP reset. Your account is engineered, not improvised.

FAQ

Asked, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. The app, the agent and all 60 listings are free. You only need an account so your persona and favorites follow you.

Which cities are covered?

Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Enugu, Kano, Calabar and Benin City — with live OpenStreetMap data refreshing the catalogue daily.

Where does the restaurant data come from?

A hand-curated catalogue of 60 spots plus daily OpenStreetMap imports. Restaurant data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Is my data safe?

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, sessions expire in 15 minutes, reset codes are HMAC-hashed with attempt limits, and secrets never touch logs or the repo.

Who built this?

Team PRiME for the DSN × BCT LLM Agent Challenge 3.0. The source is MIT-licensed on GitHub.
